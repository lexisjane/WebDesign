Banff Mountain Film Festival World Tour

Sunday, Dec. 13, 2026 at 7:00 PM - 1:00 AM

Logjam Presents, Rab and Banff & Lake Louise Tourism welcomes the Banff Mountain Film Festival World Tour for a film screening at The Wilma. Since 1976, the Banff Centre Mountain Film and Book Festival, a flagship program of Banff Centre, has been an industry leader in the celebration of Mountain Culture. We promote understanding and appreciation of the world's mountain places by creating opportunities for people to share and find inspiration in mountain experiences, ideas, and visions. Following the festival each fall, our annual World Tour reaches more than 45 countries on 7 continents, making us the largest adventure film tour on the planet.
