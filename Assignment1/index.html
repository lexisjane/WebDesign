BIRDS OF MONTANA

A brief look at some of my favorite birds of Montana

Montana is home to over 400 birds. In this short web page I will be sharing with you my top 3 favorite birds that live in Montana.

My number one favorite bird in Montana is the Killdeer. I find them to be interesting and frankly quite funny bird. Killdeer lay their eggs in open areas and will often pretend to have a broken wing to distract predators from their eggs.

The Harlequin Duck is my second favorite bird found in Montana because of its unique coloration. You can find these birds along mountain streams anywhere between April and September.

The Sandhill Crane is my third favorite bird found in Montana because of its impressive size and overall apearance. These birds have an impressive wingspan of 6-7 feet and can be found in Montana during the spring and fall migration. They also have quite a unique call that can be heard from miles away.
