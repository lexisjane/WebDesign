# BIRDS OF MONTANA

## A breif look at some of my favorite birds in Montana.

Montana is home to over 400 different bird species. In this web page you will find 3 of my favorite birds that call Montana home.

### Visual Appeal
- Which icons did you ultimately use and for what purposes? 
    - I used all 6 icons next to headers to help contextualize captions.
- Which consistent colors, values, lines, and/or shapes did you utilize and why? 
    - I stayed consistant with a monochromatic salmon but contrasted with whites.
- Which Google Fonts did you use and for what purposes? 
    - I used DM Serif Display as a header font and Nunito as a secondary paragraph font because it is easier to read in large chunks.

### Structure and Organization
- What information does your table organize, and why was a table appropriate?
    - My table organizes short facts about birds that would otherwise be lengthy in sentence form and hard to arrange neatly.
- What specific style choices helped you structure and organize your form?
    - Arranging my table in a similar manner as my bird cards helped create visual context and coherency.

### Design Goals
- What is the goal of your form, and how did it support your site's goals?
    - The goal of my form would be to gather information from users to get to know them and help improve my site.
- How does the gallery support your website's goals?
    - The gallery is a good way to showcase the featured birds beauty, which is ultimately what my site is for.
- What pattern did you use: uniform, alternating, or something else, and why?
    - My pattern sticks to a header with breathing room (no backgroun) to a shape (rectangle) and alternates accordingly with new  information.
