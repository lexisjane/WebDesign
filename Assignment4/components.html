BIRDS OF MONTANA

A brief look at some of my favorite birds of Montana

The bluebird carries the sky on his back.
Henry David Thoreau

Killdeer, Harlequin Ducks and Sandhill Cranes are three of my favorite birds in Montana because each one represents a different part of the state’s natural beauty. I love Killdeer because I find them to be an interesting and, quite frankly, a funny bird. Harlequin Ducks fascinate me because of their distinctive markings and coloration. Sandhill Cranes are another favorite of mine because of their impressive size, graceful movements, and the unique call they make which can be heard from miles away. Together, these three birds remind me of the variety of landscapes and wildlife that makes Montana such a special place.

Killdeer lay their eggs in open areas and will often pretend to have a broken wing to distract predators from their eggs.

You can find these birds along mountain streams anywhere between April and September.

These birds are an impressive size. They can range from 3 to 4 feet tall and have a wingspan of up to 7 feet.
